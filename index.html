Security

Your data stays yours

Enterprise-grade security baked in from day one — not bolted on later.

🔒

Row-Level Security

Supabase RLS ensures complete data isolation between teams. Your clients, documents, and reports are never visible to anyone outside your organization.

🛡️

Encrypted at Rest

All files and sensitive data are encrypted in storage. Every PDF attachment and document export is handled through Supabase's secure storage layer.

📋

Full Audit Log

Every action is logged on a dedicated activity log page with plain-language summaries — who did what, and when. Admins only.

Who it's for

One app, three perspectives

Smart Invoicify is a private team workspace — not a public SaaS signup. Admins invite users; everyone shares the same clients and documents.

Owners & admins

Configure & govern

Company branding, bank details, team users, backup & restore, Telegram and AI keys, and the activity log — organized in tabbed Settings.

Staff

Run day-to-day sales

Proposals, quotations, invoices, clients, catalog, expenses, and document delivery — without access to admin-only settings.

Finance & managers

Track money clearly

Payments, outstanding balances, profit by project, stock and cost visibility — plus CSV export from reports when you need spreadsheets.
