About us

Reach Solutions builds Smart Invoicify for service and project-based businesses — contractors, workshops, agencies, and small teams who need professional documents without juggling spreadsheets.

Smart Invoicify is a private team workspace: proposals, quotations, invoices, stock, purchase orders, and reports in one place. Admins invite users; there is no public self-service sign-up on licensed deployments.

Privacy

This summary describes how we handle information on the marketing site, demo sandbox, and licensed product. For contractual terms, see your license agreement with Reach Solutions.

  • Demo requests: Name, email, and optional company or phone are stored on the demo Supabase project only, with email verification before sandbox access. We do not sell demo lead data.
  • Licensed workspaces: Your business data lives in your Supabase project. Row-level security keeps each organization isolated. Files use encrypted storage through Supabase.
  • AI features (optional): When enabled by your admin, document text may be sent to your configured AI provider (e.g. Groq, Gemini, or OpenAI-compatible). You review all output before sending to clients.
  • Telegram (optional): Alerts go only to chat IDs you configure. Demo marketing leads can use a separate portal-only Telegram setting.
